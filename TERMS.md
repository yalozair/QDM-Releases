### 4. Privacy

The app does not collect personal data, does not show ads and contains no analytics or tracking. It connects to the internet only to:

- **Download** the files you ask for.
- **Check your license** with Lemon Squeezy: your license key and a device name (for example "Windows - My-PC") are sent so the key can be activated and verified.
- **Check for updates** and download helper tools from GitHub.
- **Send error reports**, only if you allow it (the app asks the first time something goes wrong, and you can turn it off in Options at any time). A report contains the error and its technical details; for a failed download, the file's link with sign-in details, keys and signatures removed, its type and size; the app version, license type, operating system and version, processor type, phone model (Android), language, whether you are online or on mobile data, and the main download settings; and a random install number that tells reports from the same device apart. Your name, your files and your computer's name are never sent. Reports go to a Google Apps Script service owned by the developer, which emails them to the developer and keeps them in a private Google Sheet, used only to find and fix problems.

**Linking a phone** works directly between your devices on your local network. When the phone is on a different network, the links you send pass through the public relay [ntfy.sh](https://ntfy.sh) end-to-end encrypted with your devices' pairing key, so neither the relay nor anyone else can read them; they are kept there for a few hours at most. **Problem reports** are sent only when you choose to send them, by email or WhatsApp, and you can read their content before sending. Your purchase data (name, email, payment) is held by Lemon Squeezy under [its privacy policy](https://www.lemonsqueezy.com/privacy).
